# Speaking feedback reference

Reviewed: 2026-10-30. Maintainer paraphrase of [IELTS Speaking format and assessment](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-speaking) and [IELTS scoring](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/your-results/ielts-scoring-in-detail). This is a feedback guide, not the official full band descriptors.

Assess four areas: continuity and organisation of speech, vocabulary, grammatical range and accuracy, and pronunciation. IELTS gives these areas equal weight. Vocabulary includes expressing meaning accurately and paraphrasing when a word is unavailable; pronunciation concerns how easily speech can be understood.

Use examples from the learner's response and recommend a focused exercise for each priority weakness. Distinguish a practice response to one question from performance across a complete speaking test. Do not treat memorized complexity or a particular accent as proof of a high score.

With plain text, comment on observable wording, grammar and organisation only. Mark pronunciation and timing/hesitation aspects of fluency as unassessed; do not assign them default scores or calculate a complete Speaking band. Audio feedback requires actual accessible audio. Any estimate from available evidence is informal practice guidance, never an official result.
